# Brand voice

Replace each rule in this file with the rules of your brand.

## Voice

- Clear and direct. Write as a practitioner who explains, not as a vendor who sells.
- Confident, not loud. Use facts instead of adjectives.
- Friendly. Use "you" and "we".

## Words

| Use                     | Do not use                          |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| "help", "make easier"   | "revolutionize", "game-changer"     |
| a specific number       | "many", "a lot", "huge"             |
| the name of the product | "our solution", "our platform"      |

## Format

- Hashtags: a maximum of 2, at the end. None on X.
- Emojis: a maximum of 1 for each post. None in the first line.
- Calls to action: one verb, for example "Read the report" or "Save your seat".

## Examples of a good first line

- "We read 400 support tickets last month. Three questions came up again and again."
- "Most teams check invoices by hand. Here is what changes when they stop."

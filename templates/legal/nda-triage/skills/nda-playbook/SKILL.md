# NDA playbook

These positions are placeholders. Replace them with the positions of your legal team.

| Topic             | Preferred (low)                                   | Acceptable (medium)                         | Escalate (high)                                   |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Direction         | Mutual                                            | One-way, we receive                         | One-way, we disclose with no protection for us    |
| Term              | 2 years or less                                   | 3 to 5 years                                | More than 5 years, or no end date                 |
| Survival          | 2 years after the term                            | Up to 5 years after the term                | Perpetual for all information                     |
| Governing law     | Our home jurisdiction                             | A neutral jurisdiction                      | A jurisdiction with no relation to either party   |
| Non-solicit       | None                                              | 12 months, employees in the project only    | More than 12 months, or all employees             |
| Carve-outs        | Public, known before, independent, third party    | One standard carve-out missing              | Two or more standard carve-outs missing           |
| Compelled release | Allowed with notice                               | Allowed with no notice                      | Not allowed                                       |
| Remedies          | Injunctive relief for both parties                | Injunctive relief for one party             | Liquidated damages or penalties                   |
| Residuals         | No residuals clause                               | Residuals limited to unaided memory         | Broad residuals clause                            |
| Non-compete       | None                                              | None                                        | Any non-compete                                   |

## Overall risk

- `high` when one finding or more is high.
- `medium` when two findings or more are medium.
- `low` in all other cases.

## Recommendation

- `sign` for low, `negotiate` for medium, `escalate` for high.

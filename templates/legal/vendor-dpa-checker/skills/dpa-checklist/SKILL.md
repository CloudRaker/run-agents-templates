# DPA checklist

Edit the requirements for your company. Each item is `pass` when the DPA meets the requirement, `fail` when the DPA has the topic but does not meet it, and `missing` when the DPA does not have the topic.

| Id                  | Requirement                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `roles`             | The DPA states that we are the controller and the vendor is the processor.                           |
| `instructions`      | The vendor processes data only on our documented instructions.                                       |
| `sub_processors`    | The DPA lists the sub-processors, or links to a list. We get 30 days notice of a new one and can object. |
| `transfer`          | Transfers out of the region use a valid mechanism: an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. |
| `security`          | The DPA lists technical and organizational measures, or links to them.                               |
| `breach_notice`     | The vendor notifies us of a personal data breach in 72 hours or less.                                |
| `audit_rights`      | We can audit the vendor, or receive a current third-party audit report, once a year.                 |
| `assistance`        | The vendor helps us answer data subject requests and do impact assessments.                          |
| `deletion`          | The vendor deletes or returns all data at the end of the service, in 30 days or less, and confirms it. |
| `confidentiality`   | Vendor staff with access to the data are bound to confidentiality.                                   |

## Overall result

- `pass` when no item is `fail` or `missing`.
- `fail` in all other cases.
